# Briefing-emails

A private, single-user tool. It reads the owner's own Gmail inbox
(read-only) to build a daily briefing for the owner. It is not offered
to anyone else.

## Privacy policy

- Access: Gmail read-only scope (`gmail.readonly`), granted by the owner only.
- Use: message headers and snippets are summarised into a briefing on the
  owner's own computer.
- Sharing: no data is sold, shared, or transferred to third parties.
- Storage: the OAuth token stays on the owner's machine. Access can be
  revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
